FAQ
Frequently asked questions

      
How do you price a website?
Each service has a starting price. The calculator includes extra pages, design and features. We agree on the final cost before work begins.
Can I order something outside the catalog?
Yes. Choose “Large custom project” and describe the features you need, or message us on Telegram.
Will the site work on my phone?
Yes. We design for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
How can I check my order status?
You’ll receive a tracking code after submitting an order through the form. Enter it under “Track an order.” If you contacted us directly on Telegram, ask for updates in your chat.
How do you use my contact details?
AFAL uses them to respond to your request and discuss the project. Message us on Telegram to request deletion of the information you submitted.
